# Vercel Design System Rules
- Canvas: Deep basalt or obsidian darks (#09090b, #081325) or pure clean light (#ffffff).
- Borders: Never use heavy solid borders. Use ultra-thin hairline borders with subtle opacity (`border border-white/[0.08]` or `border-neutral-200/80`).
- Typography: Tight tracking for big titles (`tracking-tight font-semibold`). Micro-labels must be uppercase with wide tracking (`text-[11px] uppercase tracking-[0.2em] text-neutral-400 font-medium`).
- Contrast: Strict hierarchical text contrast (pure white for H1/H2, slate-400 for subtitles, slate-600 for metadata). No rainbow colors.
