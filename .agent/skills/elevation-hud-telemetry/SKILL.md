# Elevation HUD & Route Telemetry Rules

## 1. Waypoint Milestones (Caucasus & Elbrus)
- Stage 1: `2,150 m` - Terskol Base Village (Acclimatization forest trails, Maid of the Mist falls).
- Stage 2: `3,800 m` - Gara-Bashi Barrels (High base camp, private heated refuge, glacier threshold).
- Stage 3: `4,700 m` - Pastukhov Rocks (Steep hard-packed firn ascent, extreme wind zone).
- Stage 4: `5,300 m` - The High Saddle (Sub-zero windbreak between East & West peaks).
- Stage 5: `5,642 m` - West Summit (Highest point in Europe, panoramic ridge above clouds).

## 2. Interactive HUD Overlay
- Dynamic meters counter: smoothly animate numbers on waypoint transition.
- Atmospheric data: display Oxygen Saturation level (e.g. 50% at summit), Estimated Night Temp (-15°C to -2°C), and Required Gear (Hardshell, Crampons, Ice Axe).
